Learning by Building

Building projects has become one of the most effective ways for me to understand programming and web development. Each project brings a new problem to solve, from designing interfaces to writing cleaner code and debugging unexpected issues.

I believe that becoming a better developer is not about knowing everything from the beginning. It is about staying curious, experimenting with new ideas, learning from mistakes, and continuously improving through practice.
